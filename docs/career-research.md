# Career research: from sources to portfolio decisions

**Sources checked November 11, 2026 (Eastern).** This comparison extends the existing atlas; it is not a hiring forecast or an application. Personal reflection and individual eligibility belong in Canvas.

## Research question

What work distinguishes forward deployed engineering from enterprise applied AI engineering, and what evidence could a student portfolio honestly demonstrate before claiming professional readiness?

## Evidence, inference, and assumptions

**Observed evidence:** The two original pages describe customer-facing engineering. Card B explicitly includes evaluation work. Both were opened and read; search snippets alone were not used for the cards.

**Inference:** A portfolio case study that explains a user problem, compares solutions, and records failures could communicate skills relevant to these descriptions. Neither employer promises that a student case study qualifies an applicant.

**Assumptions to test:** A reviewer can follow the source-to-decision chain without explanation; a focused case study is clearer than a gallery alone. No usability session or reviewer retry has occurred yet.
