## Emerging labels to watch

**Agent engineer**, **context engineer**, and **LLMOps engineer** are useful search terms. In this atlas they describe skill clusters rather than verified standalone vacancies: tool use and orchestration; retrieval and context management; and operating model-powered systems. Search these terms alongside applied AI engineer and software engineer, then compare actual responsibilities.

No title alone proves demand, seniority, salary, or job security. [Read the research method](/research).
