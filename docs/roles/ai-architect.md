# Applied AI Architect

> **Focus:** Systems design · **Evidence reviewed:** November 5, 2026

## What the role is for

Help teams select and design an AI solution that fits their technical and business environment.

## Skills to investigate

Architecture, integration tradeoffs, security, prototyping, and technical communication.

## Typical work product

A design proposal that explains data flow, model choices, access controls, and operating costs.

## Portfolio idea

Design a support assistant with retrieval, access boundaries, human escalation, and a cost estimate.

This project is an editorial practice suggestion, not an employer hiring requirement.

## Questions to ask before applying

- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
- How much time goes into coding, customer work, experimentation, and coordination?
- Who owns production quality, data access, and ongoing maintenance?
- What experience is required rather than preferred?

Check whether the role emphasizes pre-sales, implementation, partnerships, or enterprise architecture.
