# Forward Deployed Engineer

> **Focus:** Customer delivery · **Evidence reviewed:** November 5, 2026

## What the role is for

Build software alongside customer teams to solve a concrete operational problem.

## Skills to investigate

Software engineering, discovery interviews, API integration, deployment, and stakeholder communication.

## Typical work product

A workflow prototype, an integration plan, and a handover that a customer team can maintain.

## Portfolio idea

A case study turning a manual intake process into a reviewed AI workflow. Measure completion time and error rates.

This project is an editorial practice suggestion, not an employer hiring requirement.

## Questions to ask before applying

- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
- How much time goes into coding, customer work, experimentation, and coordination?
- Who owns production quality, data access, and ongoing maintenance?
- What experience is required rather than preferred?

Customer ambiguity and production constraints are as important as coding. Ask about travel and on-site expectations.
