# AI Research Engineer

> **Focus:** Experimental systems · **Evidence reviewed:** November 5, 2026

## What the role is for

Build systems and experiments that help researchers test and improve AI methods.

## Skills to investigate

Strong programming, machine learning fundamentals, experiment design, and infrastructure.

## Typical work product

Reproducible experiments, research tooling, or systems that accelerate investigation.

## Portfolio idea

Reproduce a small open research result and publish the setup, comparison, compute use, and limitations.

This project is an editorial practice suggestion, not an employer hiring requirement.

## Questions to ask before applying

- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
- How much time goes into coding, customer work, experimentation, and coordination?
- Who owns production quality, data access, and ongoing maintenance?
- What experience is required rather than preferred?

Research engineering is an established role being reshaped by AI; it is not a newly invented title. Requirements differ by team.
