## A field guide for a changing industry

“Forward engineer” often refers to a **forward deployed engineer**: someone who works closely with customers to turn AI capabilities into usable systems. That is one route into AI work. Application development, evaluation, architecture, research, and adoption offer other routes.

This atlas starts with **six role families** and a guide to emerging labels such as agent engineer and context engineer. It separates employer evidence from editorial learning suggestions. It is a career research resource, not a live job board or a forecast of hiring growth.
