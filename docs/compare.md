# Compare career paths

Choose by the work you enjoy, then validate against several current job descriptions. This comparison is an editorial synthesis rather than an employer requirements matrix.

| Role | Main partner | Work to practice | Useful portfolio evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Forward Deployed Engineer](/roles/forward-deployed-engineer) | Customer operators and engineers | Discover, build, integrate, hand over | Working customer-style case study |
| [Applied AI Engineer](/roles/applied-ai-engineer) | Product and engineering | Build features and evaluate quality | Deployed app with evaluation results |
| [Applied AI Architect](/roles/ai-architect) | Technical and business leaders | Design systems and explain tradeoffs | Architecture proposal and prototype |
| [Evaluation Engineer](/roles/evaluation-engineer) | Research, product, and safety | Design tests and analyze failures | Reproducible evaluation harness |
| [Research Engineer](/roles/research-engineer) | Researchers | Build experiments and research systems | Reproduction with documented methods |
| [AI Deployment Manager](/roles/ai-deployment-manager) | Delivery and business teams | Coordinate rollout and adoption | Pilot plan with measurable outcomes |

## Read beyond the title

Check the expected coding depth, customer contact, travel, production ownership, domain specialization, and experience level. A role that sounds accessible can still require substantial prior experience.

## Research a real opportunity

Save the employer URL and review date. Extract five responsibilities, separate required from preferred skills, and map each requirement to evidence you already have or a specific practice project. Compare at least three postings before drawing conclusions about a role family.
