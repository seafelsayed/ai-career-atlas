# Research & sources

**Reviewed November 5, 2026.** This is a curated starter guide, not a comprehensive labor-market study.

## How to interpret this atlas

Role profiles are editorial summaries and learning suggestions. Employer directories establish that titles appear in hiring materials; they do not establish a universal job description. The cyber evaluation listing provides a specific example of evaluation work. Openings and URLs can change.

“Emerging” includes established roles taking on new AI responsibilities. We do not claim that all listed careers are new, that hiring is growing at a measured rate, or that any title guarantees compensation or employment. There are no invented salary ranges or demand rankings.

## Update a finding

Record the employer, exact title, URL, review date, and a short paraphrase of the relevant evidence. Mark closed listings as historical examples. Keep your interpretation separate from direct employer statements. Avoid copying full job descriptions.
